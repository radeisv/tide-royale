# Assets

## Sample photos (`app/samples/`)

All six were made for this entry with an image model (photorealistic,
golden-hour coastal scenes). No real people, no scraped photos, no official
artwork.

- `01-hero.jpg` — laughing brunette woman with curly hair and sunglasses,
  sunset beach (the hero, THE HEADLINER)
- `02-admiral.jpg` — silver-haired older man in a linen suit at a marina
  (THE ADMIRAL)
- `03-outsider.jpg` — young man in a leather jacket on a motorcycle at sunset
  (THE OUTSIDER)
- `04-spotlight.jpg` — woman with long braids on a neon-lit street at night
  (THE SPOTLIGHT)
- `05-flame.jpg` — red-haired woman in a green dress on a rooftop at sunset
  (THE FLAME)
- `06-ride.jpg` — red sports car on a wooden pier at sunset (THE RIDE)
